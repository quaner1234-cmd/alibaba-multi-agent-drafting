# Earlier preparation and pump follow-up

These are historical snapshots, not a current inventory count or a sum of every
product ever drafted. Package counts, unique products, bank assets and saved drafts
are different units.

The source index recorded 1629 packages with a parsed model, 60 without, 1482 with a
video filename and 87 multi-package model groups. The v2 deferrals covered 69 variant
model groups and 11 family-prefix groups; group counts are not package counts.
Normalized exact alias equivalence was used; different model numbers were not fuzzily
merged. The platform snapshot was non-atomic during another executor's work, so a
fresh duplicate check remained the executor's responsibility before creation.

Three sample cards received local/title/preview checks; a later ten-product cohort
also received them. Image-choice and video-registration corrections were preserved
as new versions rather than rewriting original cards. These preparation checks did
not themselves create products. A preview with no broken images did not prove the
chosen first detail image actually contained the claimed model cue.

The indexed pool was a package-level screen. It did not establish that every remaining
package had a correct product category, approved condition, approved price tiers or
full execution readiness. The supplied package remains the normal trusted starting
point; observed contradictions affect the individual item, not a new full-library audit.

and reserves deeper byte/content diagnostics for real exceptions.
